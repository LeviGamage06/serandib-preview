YOUR SRI LANKAN WEDDING COMPANION

More clarity.
More room for joy.

A starting point for your conversations, your priorities and the decisions ahead.

01 / BEGIN WITH WHAT MATTERS

Before the venue.

Agree on an approximate guest count, a comfortable overall budget and the traditions you want to honour. Decide which dates are flexible before asking for quotes.

02 / ASK THE RIGHT QUESTIONS

Before you commit.

Ask for written inclusions, taxes, service charges, minimum attendance, payment dates, cancellation terms and a weather alternative. Published package prices are a starting point.

03 / CREATE SPACE FOR FAMILY

Make it meaningful.

Discuss ceremony timing with your chosen officiants and family. Plan space for attire changes, dietary needs, guest travel and accessible arrival routes.
