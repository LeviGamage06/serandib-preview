When you enquire

We use the names, contact details and wedding preferences you submit to respond to your request and manage planning conversations. Only authorised Serandib staff can access enquiries. Do not include payment card details, identity documents or sensitive information in the form.

Saved shortlists

Saved shortlists contain package selections and search details, not your name or contact details. A browser cookie helps you return to your lists on the same browser. Anyone with a shared shortlist link can view that list. Keep a copy of the link if you change devices or clear browser cookies.

WhatsApp, email & other websites

WhatsApp links prepare a message for you to review and send. Opening a draft does not tell us whether you sent it. Email, maps and hotel links open services with their own privacy practices.

Understanding prices

Venue information comes from linked sources and is checked on the dates shown. Published amounts may exclude taxes, extras or minimum-attendance requirements. We do not have live hotel availability. Listings do not imply a partnership. Confirm all terms directly before paying or booking.

Basic usage counts

We count actions such as venue searches, saved shortlists and WhatsApp link openings to improve this service. Those counters do not store names or wedding details. Temporary request limits help prevent spam. A WhatsApp click is not recorded as a confirmed enquiry.

Requests about your information

Contact serandib.enquiries@gmail.com to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have shared. We may need to verify the request before acting.
